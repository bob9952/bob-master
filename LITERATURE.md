# Literature and dataset evidence map

## Use of this document

This inventory records only sources and claims present in `summaries/chunk_001.md` through `chunk_016.md`; it is not a verified bibliography. Metadata marked "supplied" came from conversation excerpts, while page-level and Zotero status remain unknown unless stated otherwise. [chunk_001] [chunk_016]

The thesis needs sources for four distinct purposes: the 1D bin-packing problem and exact-method context, genetic and grouping genetic algorithms, constructive heuristics, and benchmark provenance/reference solutions. These purposes should not be collapsed into one citation. [chunk_002] [chunk_003] [chunk_011]

## Evidence and citation gaps to close

1. The core claim that 1D-BPP is NP-hard currently comes from the thesis brief and conversational references, not a page-verified primary or authoritative source. [chunk_001] [chunk_016]
2. The implemented method must be cited as a permutation GA with a constructive decoder, not as Falkenauer's Grouping GA; the two representations and operators are different. [chunk_001] [chunk_012]
3. Claims that First Fit decoding should outperform Next Fit, that FFD fails on triplets, or that GA should beat FFD require either direct literature pages or controlled local evidence; predictions and illustrative tables are not results. [chunk_001] [chunk_002]
4. The Rieck PDF and C++ implementations must be checked before equating Max-Rest with Worst Fit or mapping FFL/BFL/counting-sort variants to `+` and `++` names. [chunk_005] [chunk_013] [chunk_014]
5. Experiment methodology needs citations and a fixed protocol: selected instances, seed policy, independent GA runs, tuning/evaluation separation, stopping rule, hardware, timing method, and statistical summaries remain unsettled. [chunk_003] [chunk_016]
6. The Easy/Medium/Hard labels are project-specific and inconsistent across revisions; capacity thresholds alone first produced 880/508/10, while adding Wäscher and Hard28 by family produced 880/480/55. This classification needs an explicit, sourced rationale or neutral strata names. [chunk_006] [chunk_015]
7. Every claim using an "optimum" must distinguish proven optimum, current best upper bound, historical best known, and construction-guaranteed optimum. [chunk_002] [chunk_003] [chunk_016]
8. No summary reports a completed Zotero import or verified Zotero item; library status is therefore unknown for every source and should be checked before bibliography work. [chunk_001] [chunk_016]
