# Literature Digests — Study Notes Only

This folder contains **study notes** on six key papers from Nikola's Zotero
"master" collection (`QPVIL3YR`), written to help him understand the sources
better while revising his thesis introduction.

**These are NOT thesis prose and NOT translations.** They are summaries and
paraphrased extraction of key points/results for Nikola's own reading. Per
the thesis's plagiarism policy (see `CLAUDE.md` and the `plagiarism-guard`
agent), any idea from these digests that ends up in the actual Serbian thesis
text must be:

1. Re-expressed entirely in Nikola's own words and sentence structure — not
   adapted, remixed, or "find-and-replace" translated from these notes or
   from the source PDFs.
2. Checked by the `plagiarism-guard` subagent before being considered final.
3. Properly cited (author, year, page/section) per MSNR citation rules —
   the "Citable specifics" section in each digest gives the precise
   page/section locations needed for that.

## Files

- `munien-2020.md` — Munien et al. (2020), comparative metaheuristic study for 1D-BPP.
- `johnson-1974.md` — Johnson, Demers, Ullman, Garey & Graham (1974), worst-case bounds for NF/FF/BF/FFD.
- `falkenauer-1996.md` — Falkenauer (1996), the hybrid Grouping GA (GGA) paper.
- `falkenauer-delchambre-1992.md` — Falkenauer & Delchambre (1992), the original grouping-GA paper.
- `bpplib-2018.md` — Delorme, Iori & Martello (2018), the BPPLIB benchmark library paper.
- `delorme-iori-martello-2016.md` — Delorme, Iori & Martello (2016), EJOR survey of exact BPP/CSP methods.

Each digest follows the same structure: full citation, one-paragraph
overview, key points relevant to a 1D-BPP GA thesis, 2–4 citable specifics
with precise locations, and a one-to-two-sentence note on how it helps
Nikola's thesis.
